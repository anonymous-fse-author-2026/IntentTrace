Root Cause Analysis

Root Cause 1: Decision points in the model lack properly defined variable domains and corresponding guard conditions on their outgoing transitions.

Root Cause 2: The continuation workflow is improperly wired, leaving the Continue Process node and its upstream converge as dead ends that prevent reaching termination states and cause the step to be dropped from matched traces.

Root Cause 3: Extraction artifacts introduced extraneous AND branches, converges, and duplicate action nodes throughout the fulfillment and payment phases, generating numerous execution paths that contradict the source description.
