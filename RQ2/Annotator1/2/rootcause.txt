Root Cause Analysis

Root Cause 1: Variable definitions incorrectly have empty ranges instead of valid categorical or boolean values.

Root Cause 2: Outgoing edges from the XOR branches are missing their required guard expressions.

Root Cause 3: The process incorrectly models independent sequential optional steps as a chain of mutually exclusive paths, where executing any step's action bypasses all subsequent steps and jumps directly to the final converge node.
