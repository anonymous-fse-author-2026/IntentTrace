i1: To begin the process, start by placing an order and then check the customer.
i2: If the customer is "not eligible to order", inform them.
i3: Otherwise, if the customer is "eligible to order", open a new order in the ERP system.
i4: After informing the customer, the process ends.
i5: Open a new order in the ERP system and perform an availability check.
i6: Check if the product is "available". If it is not, start producing the missing parts. Otherwise, continue the process.
i7: Prepare the products for shipment.
i8: Simultaneously, complete the following steps: ship the products and send the invoice while checking for payment.
i9: If the payment is received, close the order.
i10: If the payment is not received, send a reminder.
i11: If the number of reminders is less than 3, continue the process.
i12: Otherwise, if the number of reminders is equal to 3, inform the lawyer and end the process.
